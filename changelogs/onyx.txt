2026-11-20 (12.4)
- Update the kernel to Linux 6.18.63 with KernelSU Next and
  built-in WildKernels SUSFS; no separate SUSFS module is needed.
- Fix kernel crashes when restarting audio services.
- Allow removing the owner screen-lock PIN while Secure Spaces
  are configured.
- Remove owner fingerprints when clearing the owner PIN, while
  preserving fingerprint enrollments in other profiles.
- Prevent Settings crashes when screen-lock removal is rejected.

2026-11-16 (12.4)
- Fix Global charging voltage protection stopping charging near 94%.
- Show a countdown for upcoming At a Glance calendar events, then
  "now" while the event remains active until its end.
- Sync the latest Evolution X sources, including the merged Quick
  Settings double-tap sleep and At a Glance placement fixes.
- Includes Linux 6.18.39 with KernelSU Next.

2026-11-15 (12.4)
- Match Xiaomi Global stock automatic-brightness adjustments after moving
  the slider or switching between manual and Auto mode.
- Restore Onyx front/rear light-sensor policy and panel brightness
  mapping and transition rates.
- Includes Linux 6.18.39.

2026-08-31 (11.10)
- Fix LHDC A2DP offload start/suspend loop: the AVDT codec-info field was
  capped at 32 bytes on the wire (the 45-byte internal size leaked onto the
  HCI command and the controller rejected the stream start), and the ABR
  offload command now sends the 9999999 sentinel bitrate.
- Fix camera video mode: the mi media c2 "vendor0_software" IComponentStore
  service is now labeled in SELinux service_contexts, so it can register at
  boot (video recording hung waiting for the codec).
- Fix xtra-daemon and lowi-server never starting (aGPS assistance and WiFi
  positioning were silently dead): sm8750 sepolicy lacked the loc_launcher
  domain transitions that sm8550/sm8650 carry.
- Camera power hints now reach the QSPMHAL service; camera HAL PSI
  monitoring (/proc/pressure) allowed.
- Self-hosted OTA updates: the Updater now pulls from this project's own
  feed, with downloads hosted on SourceForge.
