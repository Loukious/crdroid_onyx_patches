2026-08-31 (11.10)
- Fix LHDC A2DP offload start/suspend loop: the AVDT codec-info field was
  capped at 32 bytes on the wire (the 45-byte internal size leaked onto the
  HCI command and the controller rejected the stream start), and the ABR
  offload command now sends the 9999999 sentinel bitrate.
- Fix camera video mode: the mi media c2 "vendor0_software" IComponentStore
  service is now labeled in SELinux service_contexts, so it can register at
  boot (video recording hung waiting for the codec).
- Fix xtra-daemon and lowi-server never starting (aGPS assistance and WiFi
  positioning were silently dead): sm8750 sepolicy lacked the loc_launcher
  domain transitions that sm8550/sm8650 carry.
- Camera power hints now reach the QSPMHAL service; camera HAL PSI
  monitoring (/proc/pressure) allowed.
- Self-hosted OTA updates: the Updater now pulls from this project's own
  feed, with downloads hosted on SourceForge.
